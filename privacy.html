Privacy policy

Hermes is a private AI assistant used only by its owners (one household). It accesses Google data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive) only for the single account that authorized it.

To answer requests, relevant content is sent to an AI model provider (OpenRouter and the underlying model provider, e.g. Anthropic) for processing. Access tokens are stored on a private server. Data is never sold or shared with anyone else.

Contact: the account owner.
